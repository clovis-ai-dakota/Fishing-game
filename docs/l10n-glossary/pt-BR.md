shared glossary in `docs/localization.md`.

**Names inside sentences**: zone names after "na zona %s", club names after "o clube %s", so no
article or agreement is needed; gender-sensitive item names after a colon or in parentheses
("%1$s (pintura)", "Bloqueado: %1$s").

**Secret titles** are local idioms rather than literal translations (Rock bottom "Fundo do poço",
Buzzer beater "No apagar das luzes", Corner pocket "Caçapa do canto", Shutout "De lavada", Double
header "Dobradinha", Ladder "Escadinha"); Pinball and Grand slam stay in English.

**Plurals**: `one`, `many` and `other`; `many` adds "de" when the number comes right before a noun
("1000000 de dias").
