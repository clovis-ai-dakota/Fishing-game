Drop your line into the deep sea, steer the hook past rocks, jellies and fish heads on the way down, then hook every fish you can on the way back up. Deep Line is a fishing game of skill and collecting: the deeper you go, the rarer the fish and the bigger the catch.

HOW IT PLAYS
• Drag anywhere to steer the hook as it sinks.
• You have 4 bumps: a fish's head, a rock, driftwood or a jelly each use one. After the last one, the line turns back.
• On the way up, nudge a fish's head to hook it, until your line can't hold any more.
• Deeper fish are worth more, and Sparkling fish are worth three times as much.

200 SPECIES TO COLLECT
From the sunlit shallows to the pitch-black Abyssal Floor, four depth zones hold 200 species, each with its own card in your Dex: round, friendly fish near the surface, glowing anglers and spiny hunters far below, and one legendary fish at the very bottom. Below 300 m the water goes dark: light a Lantern, or trust your reflexes.

UPGRADE YOUR ROPE
Every catch earns points. Spend them on a longer, stronger and faster rope to reach deeper zones. Any upgrade can be refunded, so you can always try another build. Milestones reward your whole fishing career.

SECRETS OF THE DEEP
Some casts are worth far more than they look. Hidden fishing secrets are waiting to be found, and nobody will tell you what they are.

FISHING BATTLES
Challenge the computer (Easy, Normal or Hard), a friend on the same phone, or players online. Both players fish the same water, so skill decides. Five modes: Best of 5, 3-Minute Rush, Heavy Haul, Species Hunt and Deep Dive.

EVERY DAY, EVERY WEEK
• A daily reward and three daily challenges, with a forgiving streak.
• Weekly migrations bring rare species closer to your hook.
• A weekly tournament where everyone fishes the same water, with leaderboards for your friends and for everyone.

MAKE IT YOURS
Design your own boat (hull colours, sails, flags and emblems), unlock looks for your hook, line and boat, and pick a menu theme for day or night. Looks never change how the game plays.

FAIR AND QUIET
• No ads.
• Everything in the shop can also be bought with points earned by fishing.
• Usage statistics and crash reports only if you allow them.
• Fish offline; online battles and rankings need a connection.

Hand-made 3D fish and boats in a cel-shaded anime style, calm music near the surface and a darker tune in the deep.
