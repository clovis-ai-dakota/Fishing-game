# Audio credits

Every sound and music file in this folder is made from **CC0 1.0 (public domain)** assets found on
OpenGameArt.org. The licence of each was checked on its page before download. The originals were
trimmed, filtered (the bumps are low-passed to sound under water), normalised and re-encoded to
OGG Vorbis (mono sound effects, 44.1 kHz; stereo music loops, 32 kHz, made seamless by mixing each
track's last 4 seconds over its first 4). CC0 asks for no credit, but the authors deserve it: the
same list is shown in the app (Settings > Credits, from `audio/AudioCredits.kt`).

Only CC0 or public-domain audio may be added here, with its source page, author, pack and licence
recorded below and in `AudioCredits.kt` (`AudioAssetsTest` checks every file is credited).
