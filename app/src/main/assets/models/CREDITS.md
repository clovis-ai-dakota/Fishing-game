# 3D model credits

Every model here is CC0 1.0 (public domain): free to use in a sold game, credit not required but
given. They were converted from the packs' OBJ files by `tools/assets/convert_models.py` (vertex
colours sampled from the packs' textures; leaf textures shrunk to 256 px, the tree's and the bush's
recoloured in the island's greens). `ModelAssetsTest` checks that every file is listed here and in `ModelCredits`.
